KRISHIDRISHTI KRISHIDRISHTI is a smart agriculture decision-support system that helps farmers optimize irrigation, crop health, and farm productivity using real-time field data. The solution combines IoT sensors, AI-assisted analytics, and a farmer-friendly advisory platform to transform raw measurements into actionable recommendations.The proposed implementation uses an ESP32 microcontroller connected to soil moisture, temperature, humidity, rainfall, and light sensors. Sensor readings are transmitted to a backend server and stored in an SQL database. A Flask-based application processes the data, analyzes trends, and generates recommendations. Farmers can also upload crop images for AI-based pest and disease detection.Methodology:Sense → Transmit → Store → Analyze → Explain → ActField data is continuously collected and transmitted to the backend. Rule-based analysis and trend monitoring identify irrigation needs, crop stress, and environmental risks. AI models assist in pest and disease recognition, while explainable alerts provide the reasoning behind every recommendation. We have KrishiSaathi as our AI model which is proficient enough to help the farmers.Desired Outputs:• Smart irrigation recommendations based on soil and weather conditions.• Water-stress and crop-health alerts.• Pest and disease identification with suggested actions.• Harvest and yield estimation support.• Farm economics insights including cost, revenue, and profit estimation.• Multilingual advisory support through the KrishiSaathi interface.Block Diagram:  Sensors (Moisture, Temperature, Humidity, Rainfall, Light)            ↓  ESP32 Controller            ↓ Communication Layer            ↓ Flask Backend + SQL Database            ↓ Analytics & AI Engine            ↓ Recommendation & Alert System            ↓ Farmer Dashboard / KrishiSaathiKRISHIDRISHTI promotes precision agriculture by enabling data-driven decisions, reducing water wastage, improving crop management, and supporting sustainable farming practices through a scalable and low-cost technology framework.
